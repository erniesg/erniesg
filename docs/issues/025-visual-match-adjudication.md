# Adjudicate ambiguous and unresolved visual objects

depends-on: 013,021,022,023

## Provider

vm-codex

## Goal

Extend the replayable human decision loop to visual relationships so `AMBIGUOUS_VISUAL_MATCH` and `UNRESOLVED_VISUAL_OBJECT` diagnostics have bounded legal repairs instead of remaining terminal after note and reading-order ambiguities can already be resolved.

## Acceptance tests

- Every visual-match candidate has a stable identifier derived from its relationship id, source region/object ids, asset ids, and decision-schema version; candidate order or UI order cannot change its identity.
- The diagnostic inspector shows the caption, source boxes, candidate visual bounds, asset previews, score, and evidence already held in `PdfVisualRelationship` and `PdfVisualMatchCandidate`.
- Legal decisions are scoped to one diagnostic: accept one existing candidate whose complete exportable asset payload already exists; choose a bounded source-backed fallback whose complete asset already exists in the reconstruction; or classify named source objects as non-semantic decoration using the closed reason enum `page-furniture | separator-rule | decorative-ornament | background`. No decision changes a global threshold or hides a diagnostic category.
- Applying an accepted candidate constructs the same canonical figure/caption/assets relationship the deterministic matcher would have produced, updates completeness counts once, clears only that blocker, and leaves unrelated diagnostics unchanged.
- A sidecar records ids, codes, and choices only. It contains no document text, raster, asset bytes, local path, or generated description; a changed or missing candidate is reported as `STALE_HUMAN_DECISION`.
- Export provenance and `EPUB/export.json` distinguish human-adjudicated visual relationships from deterministic matches and count them by diagnostic code. A decoration decision records the exact removed source object, closed reason, old/new expected-object denominator, and resulting coverage; it never silently improves the metric.
- A new `tests/fixtures/pdf/visual-adjudication-required.pdf`, generated by the existing fixture script and recorded in its manifest, begins review-required with one ambiguous and one unresolved visual, becomes ready after bounded complete-asset choices, exports real assets, and reproduces byte-identical EPUBs when the decision JSON is replayed.

## Exact-head definition of done

- The fixture starts blocked, cannot accept an incomplete/non-candidate asset, becomes ready only through legal scoped decisions, and replays to identical graph and EPUB bytes at the exact PR head.
- Unit, importer browser, full validation, and exact-head evidence checks pass without suppressing a visual diagnostic, widening thresholds, or updating a golden result to accept semantic loss.
- Decision sidecars and receipts contain only stable ids, closed reasons, denominator changes, and provenance; they contain no text, image bytes, paths, secrets, or model-generated decisions.
- From a clean checkout of the immutable PR head, the canonical evidence manifest records `commit` equal to that head, `dirty: false`, `result: passed`, and every required lane; a post-lane clean-worktree check proves validation did not modify tracked or generated source files.
- Replaying `tests/fixtures/pdf/visual-adjudication-required.pdf` with its named sidecar produces byte-stable canonical and EPUB artifact hashes bound to the source, decision schema, profile, and toolchain in the receipt.

## Allowed secrets

None. Documents, candidate images, and sidecars remain local.

## Artifact outputs

Stable visual candidate ids, visual adjudication resolutions and applier, closed decoration reasons and denominator provenance, inspector controls, stale-decision handling, provenance/manifest integration, named fixture, and replay evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop before uploading visuals, accepting a model suggestion without a human choice, allowing free-form sidecar patches, marking a possibly meaningful object decorative without naming it, or widening a one-document decision into a corpus rule.

## Human clarification protocol

If the existing candidate model cannot express the correct bounded visual, report the missing source identifiers and smallest schema addition; do not add a coordinate-editing canvas as part of this issue.

## Recommended response

Reuse the issue-022 pre-gate decision applier and issue-021 overlays, adding visual-specific candidate identity and resolutions while keeping the downstream canonical graph unchanged.

## Trade-offs

Per-document repair is slower than a learned matcher but produces trustworthy ground truth and a safe evidence corpus for later rule or model evaluation.

## Free-form response

The current decision union resolves notes, marker classification, reading order, and dismissible diagnostics, but it contains no visual resolution. The two visual error codes therefore have no legal UI action even though their candidates and evidence already exist.
