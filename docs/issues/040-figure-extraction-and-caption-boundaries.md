# Stop dropping figures and stop captions absorbing body prose

depends-on: 013,025,033,038,042

## Provider

vm-codex

## Goal

Deliver the figures a paper contains, and keep a caption's boundary exactly where the source puts it. A dropped diagram is invisible to every current metric, and a caption that runs on into body text corrupts two paragraphs at once — the caption and the paragraph it stole from.

## Observed failure

On a two-column paper whose `textCoverage` is 0.99988, page two carries a full-page-width flowchart. In the generated EPUB the figure is absent while its caption survives. The document declares seven figures and renders none of them.

On the same page the caption then absorbed the following column's opening sentence, so the output reads:

```
Fig. 1: An overview of the different phases of filtering we go through to collect a GITS
evaluation set internal environment (e.g. logging, which is sensitive business data and
must be stored accordingly).
```

The body paragraph that sentence belongs to now terminates mid-sentence at "interacts with the Google". A third defect on the same page splits a sentence spanning the column break into two paragraph blocks.

## Acceptance tests

- A figure whose source objects form a bounded component set is exported as a packaged asset with source hash, complete object and region ids, bounded geometry, non-empty dimensions, and a caption relationship. A multi-fragment vector diagram is assembled only when one connected bounded component set validates.
- A figure that cannot be materialized is review-required with bounded candidate evidence and a named diagnostic. It is never silently absent while its caption is emitted; a caption without its figure is itself a blocking condition.
- Caption extent is proved from source geometry and typography, not from proximity alone. A caption ends at its own block boundary; text in a different column, a different lane, or a different typographic run is never appended to it.
- A body paragraph whose continuation was claimed by a caption is restored: the paragraph ends where the source ends it, and no sentence terminates mid-clause because a neighbouring float took its text.
- A sentence spanning a column break joins into one paragraph when its source lines prove continuity, and the join is recorded in the line-boundary ledger.
- The figure, caption-boundary, and column-join properties each have a named checkpoint under issue 038 that fails on current `main` and passes when this lands.
- Fixtures cover: a full-width vector diagram above a caption, a multi-fragment diagram, a caption adjacent to a second column's prose, a sentence spanning a column break, and a raster figure. Each is generated by the fixture script and recorded in the manifest.

## TDD sequence

1. **Red:** add the fixtures above; preserve failures for the absent figure, the over-extended caption, the truncated body paragraph, and the split sentence.
2. **Green:** prove caption extent from block geometry and typography, then repair figure component assembly so a bounded diagram materializes or fails closed with evidence.
3. **Red then green:** add the column-break join and prove it through the line-boundary ledger.
4. **Refactor:** share the figure materializer with the existing visual path only after provenance and byte-stability assertions hold.

## Exact-head definition of done

- The observed page renders its flowchart, its caption ends where the source ends it, the neighbouring paragraph is whole, and the column-spanning sentence is one paragraph.
- No figure is omitted without a blocking diagnostic naming why.
- From a clean checkout of the immutable PR head, `scripts/agent-evidence --all` records `commit` equal to that head, `dirty: false`, and `result: passed`.

## Artifact outputs

Proved caption extents, repaired figure component assembly, column-break sentence joins, and checkpoints for each under issue 038.

## Stop conditions

Stop before emitting a caption whose figure is absent without a blocking diagnostic, extending a caption across a lane boundary, or joining a column break the ledger cannot prove.

## Human clarification protocol

If a diagram's fragments cannot form one bounded component set, confirm that review-required is preferred over a partial render.

## Recommended response

Prove caption extent from block geometry and typography rather than proximity, since proximity is what let a caption cross into the next column.

## Trade-offs

Failing closed on an unprovable figure costs an export, but silently dropping seven of seven figures while emitting their captions is worse for a reader and invisible to metrics.

## Free-form response

A dropped diagram plus a caption that stole the next paragraph corrupts three things at once and none of them appear in any audit counter.
