# Exclude page furniture from body flow and account for every excluded run

depends-on: 037,038

## Provider

vm-codex

## Goal

Keep running heads, page numbers, journal mastheads, arXiv identifiers, footer rules, and watermark furniture out of the reading flow of every paper, without an allowlist of known journals, while accounting for every excluded run so nothing recoverable is silently discarded.

## Observed failure

Corpus papers carry per-page furniture — repeated running heads, incrementing page numbers, arXiv stamps rotated in the left margin — that today either leaks into body paragraphs mid-sentence or is dropped with no record. A page number that lands inside a paragraph corrupts the prose stratum; a dropped acknowledgment line that merely *looked* like furniture is an invisible loss. Both failures are unmeasured: no diagnostic distinguishes furniture exclusion from text loss.

## Acceptance tests

- A run repeated across pages in the same page band with identical or incrementing-numeral text is classified as furniture from geometry and repetition evidence alone, for any language and numeral system, with no journal-name or keyword allowlist.
- A rotated margin stamp (e.g. an arXiv identifier) is classified from its rotation and margin band, not its text content.
- Every excluded run is recorded in the STRUCT graph as an accounted furniture block with its evidence, never silently deleted; text coverage accounting treats furniture as covered-by-exclusion, distinct from covered-in-flow and from lost.
- A run that appears once (a genuine sentence that happens to sit low on one page) is never classified as furniture; single-occurrence margin text falls to review with bounded evidence instead of exclusion.
- First-page title-block text (title, authors, affiliations) is never claimed by the furniture classifier.
- Furniture contamination of body flow is a named per-document counter, and a checkpoint under issue 038 fails on current `main` for a fixture with contaminated flow and passes when this lands.
- Fixtures cover: repeated running head, incrementing page numbers in three numeral systems, rotated arXiv stamp, a footer rule with page number, a one-off margin note that must not be excluded, and a two-column paper whose furniture band abuts body text. Each is generated by the fixture script and recorded in the manifest.

## TDD sequence

1. **Red:** add the fixtures above; preserve failures for the contaminated paragraph, the dropped one-off margin note, and the missing accounting counter.
2. **Green:** classify furniture from cross-page repetition, band geometry, and rotation; route exclusions into accounted furniture blocks with evidence.

4. **Refactor:** only after byte-stability of the classification across two identical runs is asserted.

## Exact-head definition of done

- The fixture paragraphs contain no page numbers or running heads, the one-off margin note survives to review, and every excluded run is queryable from the STRUCT graph with evidence.
- From a clean checkout of the immutable PR head, `scripts/agent-evidence --all` records `commit` equal to that head, `dirty: false`, and `result: passed`.

## Allowed secrets

None.

## Artifact outputs

A furniture classifier with repetition/band/rotation evidence, accounted furniture blocks in the STRUCT graph, a per-document contamination counter, and an issue 038 checkpoint.

## Stop conditions

Stop before excluding any single-occurrence run, matching furniture by journal name or keyword, or deleting an excluded run without an accounted block.

## Human clarification protocol

If a paper's furniture band overlaps genuine footnote bodies, confirm that footnotes win and furniture classification defers to the note stratum.

## Recommended response

Classify from cross-page repetition and geometry first; repetition is the one signal that generalises across journals, languages, and numeral systems.

## Trade-offs

Repetition evidence needs at least two pages, so single-page documents keep their furniture unless band geometry alone is decisive; that is acceptable because single-page papers are rare and review-required is the declared fallback.

## Free-form response

Furniture is the noise floor under every other stratum: until it is excluded and accounted, prose continuity, caption extent, and note association are all measured against contaminated text.
