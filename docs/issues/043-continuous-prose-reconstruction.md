# Deliver continuous prose across lines, columns, and pages

depends-on: 024,035,038,042

## Provider

vm-codex

## Goal

Reconstruct body prose as the paragraphs the author wrote: joined across line breaks, column breaks, and page breaks when source evidence proves continuity, with discretionary hyphens resolved or explicitly left unresolved, and with nothing promoted to structure the source does not prove.

## Observed failure

Exported EPUBs show paragraphs that terminate mid-clause at a column break, sentences split into two blocks by a page turn, hyphen-ated words carried into the flow as two fragments, and — worst — source content flattened into headings: literal Markdown syntax and template placeholders from the source text promoted to heading structure with no typographic evidence. On one corpus paper an equation region flowed as garbled prose (`√√ (` fragments interleaved with words) because the prose reconstructor claimed runs the equation stratum owned.

## Acceptance tests

- A sentence spanning a column break joins into one paragraph when line geometry, typography, and language-agnostic continuation evidence prove continuity; the join is recorded in the line-boundary ledger with its evidence.
- A sentence spanning a page break joins under the same proof, and the join survives furniture exclusion under issue 042 (the intervening running head never enters the paragraph).
- A discretionary line-end hyphen is resolved to the joined word only when the joined form is attested elsewhere in the document or the split form is not; otherwise the hyphen is preserved and the unresolved resolution is a recorded diagnostic, never a silent guess.
- A run owned by an equation, table, caption, or figure stratum is never claimed by prose reconstruction; ownership conflicts are named diagnostics with both claimants' evidence.
- Text that merely looks like markup — `#`, `**`, `{placeholder}`, numbered-list syntax — is never promoted to heading, bold, or list structure without independent typographic or geometric evidence; a fixture with literal Markdown in body prose renders it as prose characters.
- Paragraph boundaries match source paragraph spacing and indentation evidence; two source paragraphs are never merged because their gap is small, and one is never split because its lines are far apart, without the ledger recording why.
- Checkpoints under issue 038 cover the column join, page join, hyphen resolution, and markup non-promotion, each failing on current `main` for its fixture and passing when this lands.
- Fixtures cover: column-break sentence, page-break sentence, hyphenated break with attested joined form, hyphenated break with unattested joined form, literal Markdown in prose, a template-placeholder line, and prose adjacent to an owned equation region. Each is generated by the fixture script and recorded in the manifest.

## Artifact outputs

Proved cross-column and cross-page joins in the line-boundary ledger, attestation-based hyphen resolution, stratum ownership enforcement, markup non-promotion, and issue 038 checkpoints for each.

## Stop conditions

Stop before joining without ledger proof, resolving a hyphen without attestation, promoting markup-shaped text without typographic evidence, or letting prose claim a run another stratum owns.

## Human clarification protocol

If hyphen attestation is ambiguous in both directions for a high-frequency word, confirm that preserving the source hyphen is preferred over a dictionary dependency.

## Recommended response

Enforce stratum ownership first: most observed prose corruption is other strata's content leaking in, and joins are only provable once prose contains only prose.

## Trade-offs

Attestation-based hyphen resolution leaves rare words hyphenated where a dictionary would fix them, but it stays language-agnostic and never invents a word form the source cannot attest.

## Free-form response

Continuous prose is the product a reader actually consumes; every earlier stratum exists so that this one can be both fluent and provably the author's text.
