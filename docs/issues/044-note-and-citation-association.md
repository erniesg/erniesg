# Resolve every footnote, endnote, and citation to a verified target

depends-on: 038,042,043

## Provider

vm-codex

## Goal

Give every note marker a verified body, every citation a verified bibliography entry, and every figure/table/equation reference a verified target, so the exported EPUB's links are clickable and correct — and where the source leaves an association genuinely ambiguous, keep the marker visible without inventing a destination.

## Observed failure

The corpus audit's two largest blockers are note association: `UNRESOLVED_NOTE_REFERENCE` (6,882 occurrences) and `AMBIGUOUS_NOTE_MATCH` (2,184) across 72 papers. Exported EPUBs show citation markers rendered as plain text with no link, references sections present but unanchored, and note markers inside table cells and captions that resolve to nothing. Markers that fall inside floats are missed entirely.

## Acceptance tests

- A note marker (superscript numeral, symbol, or bracketed token, in any numeral system) resolves to its note body through geometry, numbering-sequence, and typography evidence; the resolved pair is a STRUCT relationship with status `matched`, a backlink, and evidence on both ends.
- A citation token (`[12]`, `(Author, year)`, superscript, or range like `[3–5]`, including multi-citation groups) anchors each constituent to its typed bibliography entry; ranges expand to every member.
- Markers inside table cells, captions, and footnote bodies themselves participate in resolution; a fixture with a citation inside a caption resolves it.
- An ambiguous association is never silently guessed: it stays a visible marker with a `ambiguous` relationship carrying every candidate and its evidence, and the EPUB renders the marker without a false destination.
- No link in the exported EPUB points at a nonexistent anchor: an EPUB-level verifier walks every internal href to its target and fails the export on any dangling link.
- The corpus counters for unresolved and ambiguous note references are re-measured on the fixture corpus and reported before/after in the PR evidence; the resolved-association rate must rise with zero false-link regressions on fixtures with known ground truth.
- Checkpoints under issue 038 cover marker-to-body, citation-to-entry, in-float markers, and the dangling-link verifier, each failing on current `main` for its fixture and passing when this lands.
- Fixtures cover: numeric footnotes, symbol footnotes, endnotes, non-Latin numeral markers, citation ranges, author-year citations, a citation inside a caption, a marker inside a table cell, and a genuinely ambiguous marker that must stay unresolved. Each is generated by the fixture script and recorded in the manifest.

## Artifact outputs

Evidence-backed note and citation relationships in the STRUCT graph, typed bibliography entries, an EPUB dangling-link verifier gate, before/after association counters in PR evidence, and issue 038 checkpoints.

## Stop conditions

Stop before emitting a link whose target is not verified, collapsing an ambiguous association to one candidate without decisive evidence, or dropping a marker because its body was not found.

## Human clarification protocol

If a paper's numbering restarts per page or per chapter and sequence evidence conflicts with geometry, present both candidates and ask which convention the corpus should default to.

## Recommended response

Resolve by numbering sequence first with geometry as tiebreaker: sequences are global evidence and survive the float and column cases that defeat proximity matching.

## Trade-offs

Fail-closed ambiguity keeps some markers unlinked that a proximity guess would connect correctly, but a false citation link misleads a reader silently, and 2,184 ambiguous matches make guessing a systematic corruption source.

## Free-form response

Notes and citations are the paper's own hyperlink graph; until it round-trips verified, the EPUB is a flattened photocopy of a linked document.
