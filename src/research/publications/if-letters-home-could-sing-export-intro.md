# Abstract

This research explores the intersection of cultural heritage, interactive design, and embodied experience to transform static historical Chinese diaspora correspondence into dynamic, living encounters. Focusing on *qiao pi* (侨批), or “letters home,” held in the National Museum of Singapore’s collection, it centres Hainanese women’s voices and brings their “history from below” to life through heart rate-synchronised sound experiences.

**Keywords:** qiao pi, cultural heritage, biometric integration, Chinese diaspora, new museology, counter-narratives, embodied experience

This research was supported by the **National Heritage Board** through the National Museum of Singapore’s 2024 Research Fellowship programme.
